## Findings ledger

- **H-01 — High; historical CI; high confidence.** At the pinned SHA, [CI](https://github.com/knarayanareddy/Haven-build/actions/runs/41296897959), [production checks](https://github.com/knarayanareddy/Haven-build/actions/runs/41296897946) and [deployment pipeline](https://github.com/knarayanareddy/Haven-build/actions/runs/41296897970) ended `failure`. [CI:32–56](https://github.com/knarayanareddy/Haven-build/blob/b8dd8ced6228051f47797644f06271826b1227ff/.github/workflows/ci.yml#L32-L56) includes types and lint. Failed steps and causes are unknown; inspect each run, reproduce and rerun without weakening gates. These are historical run conclusions, not proof of a current defect or deployment.
- **H-02 — High; confirmed static; high confidence.** [Supabase integration:40–58](https://github.com/knarayanareddy/Haven-build/blob/b8dd8ced6228051f47797644f06271826b1227ff/.github/workflows/supabase-integration.yml#L40-L58) says “Skipping live RLS” when credentials are absent and conditions the test on readiness. A successful workflow alone therefore does not prove live isolation. Require independently recorded execution for release; no inference is made about whether a particular run skipped.
- **H-03 — Medium; confirmed static; high confidence.** [Supabase integration:11–17](https://github.com/knarayanareddy/Haven-build/blob/b8dd8ced6228051f47797644f06271826b1227ff/.github/workflows/supabase-integration.yml#L11-L17) selects CLI `latest`. Local reset behavior can drift without a repository change; pin a verified version and review upgrades.
- **H-04 — Medium; confirmed static; high confidence.** [Fall handler:97–110](https://github.com/knarayanareddy/Haven-build/blob/b8dd8ced6228051f47797644f06271826b1227ff/supabase/functions/fn-fall-escalation/index.ts#L97-L110) checks `loc.precise_longitude && loc.precise_latitude`: valid zero latitude or longitude fails the condition. Consequently this handler omits the location URL for those coordinates even when a fresh row exists. Use explicit null and finite/range checks; verify notification data with zero-coordinate fixtures. Actual frequency and clinical impact are unverified.
- **H-05 — Medium; confirmed static; high confidence.** [Offline queue:6–22](https://github.com/knarayanareddy/Haven-build/blob/b8dd8ced6228051f47797644f06271826b1227ff/apps/elder/src/services/sqliteOfflineQueue.ts#L6-L22) catches every `alter table` error as “Column already exists”. Other SQLite failures can be hidden and leave an upgrade incomplete. Distinguish already-present columns from operational errors and verify upgraded schemas and fault propagation; no device failure was reproduced.
- **H-06 — Medium; confirmed static; medium confidence in impact.** [Offline queue:61–74](https://github.com/knarayanareddy/Haven-build/blob/b8dd8ced6228051f47797644f06271826b1227ff/apps/elder/src/services/sqliteOfflineQueue.ts#L61-L74) selects queued or stale-processing rows, but its later update accepts `status = 'queued' or coalesce(last_attempt_at, '1970') < ?` without requiring `processing` for the stale branch. If a selected stale row becomes `failed` before that update, it can be reclaimed. Potential quarantine bypass depends on concurrent callers; reproduce the interleaving and guard both status branches atomically.

- **H-07 — High; confirmed static; high confidence.** The deployment's [post-release monitoring step:259–265](https://github.com/knarayanareddy/Haven-build/blob/b8dd8ced6228051f47797644f06271826b1227ff/.github/workflows/deploy.yml#L259-L265) announces a 30-minute Sentry monitoring loop, executes `sleep 10`, then prints “Zero exception drift detected.” There is no metric query in that step; its success cannot establish monitored health. Actual production execution was not observed. Replace simulated monitoring with measured, timestamped checks and explicit unavailable-data failure; do not label waiting or unconditional output as successful observation.

## Five-discipline assessment

**Correctness/code.** H-04–H-06 concern sampled source, not observed live outcomes. The fall code otherwise scopes notification recipients to active, consenting relationships and checks the persisted `send_error` before counting submissions ([handler:147–181](https://github.com/knarayanareddy/Haven-build/blob/b8dd8ced6228051f47797644f06271826b1227ff/supabase/functions/fn-fall-escalation/index.ts#L147-L181)); a submission is not proof a person received or acted on an alert. OCR result correctness, full queue delivery and clinical decision logic remain uninspected.

**Automation/testing.** [Root manifest:7–19](https://github.com/knarayanareddy/Haven-build/blob/b8dd8ced6228051f47797644f06271826b1227ff/package.json#L7-L19) declares edge, browser and live-RLS tests. Test names do not prove they ran or exercise real services. H-01 and H-02 require run-level and negative-control evidence rather than aggregate green status.

**DevOps/operations.** [Deployment workflow:1–18](https://github.com/knarayanareddy/Haven-build/blob/b8dd8ced6228051f47797644f06271826b1227ff/.github/workflows/deploy.yml#L1-L18) has push/manual triggers and serialized deployment. H-07 exposes a false health-observation signal. Environment protections, actual deployment execution, signing, alert delivery and restore results were not verified. H-03 is a reproducibility issue, not an asserted compromise.

**OWASP/security.** Authz [validates users via `getUser`](https://github.com/knarayanareddy/Haven-build/blob/b8dd8ced6228051f47797644f06271826b1227ff/supabase/functions/_shared/authz.ts#L58-L79), and [queries active consent and denies relationship errors or missing permissions](https://github.com/knarayanareddy/Haven-build/blob/b8dd8ced6228051f47797644f06271826b1227ff/supabase/functions/_shared/authz.ts#L128-L177). This does not establish that every endpoint invokes these helpers or that RLS, storage and revocation work end to end. Injection resistance, secrets in artifacts/logs, locked dependency integrity, retention and erasure of health/location data are **unverified risks**, not confirmed exploits.

**UI/UX/accessibility.** [Medication screen:129–157](https://github.com/knarayanareddy/Haven-build/blob/b8dd8ced6228051f47797644f06271826b1227ff/apps/elder/src/screens/MedicationScanScreen.tsx#L129-L157) includes button labels, minimum touch sizing and a polite live region; [lines 74–82](https://github.com/knarayanareddy/Haven-build/blob/b8dd8ced6228051f47797644f06271826b1227ff/apps/elder/src/screens/MedicationScanScreen.tsx#L74-L82) distinguish review-required from added results. These source provisions do not prove screen-reader announcements, legibility or safe comprehension on devices. Permission denial, failed upload, OCR uncertainty and offline fallbacks need supervised elder/carer testing.
